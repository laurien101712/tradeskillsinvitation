Straight answers

Before you book

Am I too old?

“anyone I've spoked to says there looking for school leavers” — boards.ie

There's no age limit on apprenticeships, but some employers do favour school-leavers. That's why meeting a working tradesperson matters — and why these tasters are built for adults.

Can I afford to switch?

“Make sure you have a chunk saved up to get you through the first year.” — boards.ie

The taster costs €35–€45, not a career. Apprentice pay is low in year one, so people who've switched say to have savings set aside. Use the evening to decide whether it's worth it.

I've never used tools. Is that OK?

“my capability was doubted because of my female name on an application” — Totally Dublin / WITNI

Yes. No experience is needed. Women-only evenings run for every trade.

I trained abroad. Does this help?

“the qualifications they have gained overseas are not recognised by Irish authorities” — Dermot Carey, CIF, Construction Business

Overseas qualifications aren't always recognised in Ireland. Your tradesperson can talk you through what to check.

What happens after the taster?

You leave with a next-step sheet covering the relevant apprenticeship and how to approach employers.
